## 7. Board meanings

- Full Forecast Boards: every player/stat series generated by the simulator; market-blind; used for calibration; **not** bet recommendations.
- Market-Lined Pick Boards: model's more probable OVER/UNDER side at the consensus posted line; probability-first; **not** EV-filtered.
- EV Play Board: only price-sensitive rows passing conservative EV rules.

The final production handoff ZIP now includes:

## 8. Important architecture rule

Layer 1 is now permanently market-blind in `run_live`. Mainline sportsbook data may be retained as pricing/challenger context, but it no longer overwrites the football simulation distributions.

Actual results, grades, closing lines and CLV remain the separate Trading Agent's Dataset-B responsibility.
