# NFL Player Props Commands Playbook

## /print-nfl-player-prop-boards
Reads a frozen handoff ZIP and prints the main board stack.

## /qc-nfl-player-handoff
Checks manifest, files, timestamps, probabilities, prices, and columns.

## /write-nfl-full-player-boards
Prints full player forecast boards only.

## /rank-nfl-player-prop-ev
Prints positive-EV player prop candidates only.

## /review-nfl-player-risk-room
Summarizes hard flags, role risk, price risk, correlation risk, and top disagreements.

## /capture-nfl-player-prop-close
Records close-proxy evidence for selected/top candidates.

## /grade-nfl-player-props
Grades completed player prop results after final stats are supplied or verified.

## /build-nfl-player-prop-recap
Builds the permanent Dataset B recap.
