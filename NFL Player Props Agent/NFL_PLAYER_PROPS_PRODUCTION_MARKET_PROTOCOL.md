# NFL Player Props Production Market Protocol

Production markets are generated from the same opportunity engine, but add efficiency variance.

Markets:

```text
WR/TE receiving yards
RB rushing yards
QB passing yards
RB receiving yards
```

Production-board audit must distinguish:

- volume edge,
- efficiency edge,
- explosive-play dependence,
- quarterback dependence,
- matchup dependence,
- negative game-script risk,
- blowout risk,
- market price quality.

Production props require stronger risk-room discipline than pure opportunity props.
