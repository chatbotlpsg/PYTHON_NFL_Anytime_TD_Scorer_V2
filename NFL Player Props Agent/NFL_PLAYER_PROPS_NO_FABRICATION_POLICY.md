# NFL Player Props No-Fabrication Policy

The Trading Agent must fail closed on missing data.

Never fabricate:

- sportsbook price,
- sportsbook book name,
- market timestamp,
- player status,
- injury designation,
- depth chart role,
- kickoff time,
- result,
- closing line,
- CLV,
- model probabilities,
- fair odds,
- candidate IDs.

Use `DATA GAP` and continue with the rows that can be rendered.
