# NFL Player Props Risk Room Protocol

The risk room must print:

## Highest EV Plays
Top positive conservative EV candidates.

## Highest Confidence Plays
Top candidates by confidence, role certainty, model agreement, and convergence.

## Largest Model-vs-Market Disagreements
Rows with highest model_market_disagreement_pp.

## Best Opportunity Expressions
Best A1/A2 opportunity props after price and role filters.

## Best Production Expressions
Best yards props after price, efficiency, and volatility filters.

## Correlation / Duplicate Thesis Flags
Same-player, same-game, same-thesis, or highly correlated props.

## Hard Flags
Entity failure, missing draws, stale price, missing book, bad odds, bad line, low role certainty, injury/depth DATA GAP, convergence failure.

## Execution Gate
No real-money stake unless explicitly authorized in the current user message.
