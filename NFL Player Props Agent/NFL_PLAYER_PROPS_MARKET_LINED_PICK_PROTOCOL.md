# NFL Player Props Market-Lined Pick Board Protocol

Market-lined pick boards are probability-first boards against actual posted market lines. They are not EV boards.

Print:

1. All Market-Lined Pick Board
2. Passing Market Pick Board
3. Rushing Market Pick Board
4. Receiving Market Pick Board

Columns:

```text
Date | Time | Game | Player | Team | Opp | Market | Consensus Line | Model Side | Model Prob | Fair Odds | Best Posted Price | Best Book | Book Count | Price Status | Role Certainty | Gate
```

Gate label:

```text
DIRECTIONAL_FORECAST_NOT_EV_PLAY
```

unless the row also qualifies through the EV board.
