# NFL Player Props Opportunity Market Protocol

Opportunity markets are the flagship alpha layer.

## Tier A1

```text
WR/TE receptions
RB rushing attempts
QB pass attempts
```

## Tier A2

```text
QB completions
RB receptions
```

Interpretation checklist:

- projected play volume,
- pass/run decision process,
- player role certainty,
- depth chart stability,
- injury or inactive risk,
- QB state,
- game-script distribution,
- market line/price quality,
- conservative EV,
- buy-to/hard-stop validity.
