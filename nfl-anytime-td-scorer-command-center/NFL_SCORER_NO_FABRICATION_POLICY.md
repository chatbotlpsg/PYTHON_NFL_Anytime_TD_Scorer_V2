```text
current sportsbook price
parlay / SGP payout
closing price
injury status
lineup status
availability status
result
market timestamp
book name
joint probability
fair price
```

If missing, write:

```text

PRICE REQUIRED
NOT YET TRADEABLE
NO_JOINT_PROBABILITY_AVAILABLE
```

Do not convert most-probable into best-bet language unless the exact sportsbook price creates positive EV.
